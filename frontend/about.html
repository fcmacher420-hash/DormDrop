About DormDrop · Investor briefing

A simpler way to connect students with the products they need.

DormDrop is building a trusted online marketplace where students can discover useful campus essentials and local or international suppliers can reach student communities.

Why we are building

A marketplace made for student life

The challenge

Students often look across scattered channels to find everyday essentials, while suppliers need a clearer way to present their products to campus communities.

Our vision

We want DormDrop to become a trusted bridge between students and dependable suppliers, starting with the needs of campus life and growing as each community comes on board.

What we are building

A marketplace for browsing products by category, reviewing seller listings, asking questions directly, and moving toward a purchase in one place.

The opportunity

Start with campus needs. Grow with the community.

DormDrop is designed to serve student buyers and suppliers in one place, with room to add campuses, broaden product selection, and strengthen delivery and seller services over time.

For studentsDiscover everyday essentials and compare available listings.
For suppliersShowcase products to a focused student audience.
For communitiesBuild more convenient connections between campus demand and supply.
